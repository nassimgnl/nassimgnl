## SELECTED PROJECTS

### 01 — Global Tactical Asset Allocation (GTAA)

[![Repository](https://img.shields.io/badge/VIEW%20PROJECT-GitHub-174A55?style=flat-square&logo=github&logoColor=white)](https://github.com/nassimgnl/global-tactical-asset-allocation)

Development of a quantitative tactical asset allocation model based on a diversified ETF universe.

**Key features:**
- Global ETF universe and financial market data analysis.
- Momentum and trend-following indicators.
- Tactical asset allocation and portfolio construction.
- Monthly portfolio rebalancing.
- Risk management framework.
- Historical backtesting and performance analysis.
- Benchmark comparison against a traditional 60/40 portfolio.

**Technologies:** Python · Pandas · NumPy · Financial Modelling

### 02 — Discounted Cash Flow (DCF) Valuation Model

Development of a financial valuation framework for international publicly listed companies.

**Key features:**
- Five-year financial projections.
- Free Cash Flow modelling.
- WACC calculation (Cost of Equity, Cost of Debt and Beta).
- Terminal Value estimation.
- Sensitivity analysis (WACC / Perpetual Growth).
- Intrinsic valuation and investment analysis.

**Technologies:** Excel · Financial Modelling · Corporate Finance
